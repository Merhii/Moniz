Moniz Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Moniz has no accounts and no servers of its own. Everything you enter — your holdings, your spending, your zakat settings — is stored on your device, encrypted, and is never uploaded anywhere. Moniz makes no attempt to identify you.

What Moniz stores, and where

Moniz stores the following on your device only:

  • Holdings you record — gold, silver and cash, with amounts, purities, dates and notes
  • Money entries you log, their categories, the wallets they came from, and any repeating rules you set up
  • Zakat settings, and a record of zakat you mark as paid
  • Snapshots of your portfolio value over time, used to draw the charts
  • Preferences: display currency, theme, notification choices, reminder times
  • An optional PIN, if you turn on the app lock

This data is written to encrypted storage on the device. It is not transmitted to the developer or to any third party, and no copy of it exists outside your device.

What leaves your device

Moniz makes network requests to exactly two services, both of them public and neither of them operated by the developer:

  • api.gold-api.com — to look up the current gold and silver price
  • open.er-api.com — to look up currency exchange rates

These are plain price lookups. Nothing about you or your holdings is included in them. No account identifier, no device identifier, and no API key is sent, because Moniz has none. As with any internet request, the operators of those services may record your IP address in their own server logs; their handling of that is governed by their own policies, not this one.

What Moniz does not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, usage tracking or behavioural profiling
  • No crash or diagnostic reporting to the developer
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data — there is nothing collected to sell

Notifications

Reminders — zakat due dates, price alerts, the end-of-day nudge — are generated on your device and scheduled locally. Moniz does not use push notifications, so no notification token or message content is sent to any server. You can turn any of them off in Settings, or revoke the notification permission in Android or iOS settings.

Security

Moniz's local databases are encrypted with AES. The encryption key is held in the platform's secure credential store — the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain — rather than in the app's own files. You can additionally require a PIN or your device biometrics to open the app, from Settings.

No security measure is absolute: someone with unlocked physical access to your device, or with administrator access to it, may be able to reach data stored on it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Moniz, or clearing its storage from your device's app settings, permanently deletes everything it holds. There is no server-side copy to request the deletion of, and no way for the developer to recover it for you. If your data matters to you, keep your own record of it.

Children

Moniz is a personal finance tool and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.
